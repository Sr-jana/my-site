Hi, I'm Srijana (Sri).

I'm a data analyst who loves turning messy data into meaningful insights. My background spans finance, marketplace analytics, customer behaviour insights, and digital marketing, but the common thread across everything I do is data.

I've completed my Master of Business Analytics at Deakin University, where I strengthened my skills in SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and applied statistics. I also work as a Finance Analyst, bringing analytical thinking into financial operations, automation, reporting, and process optimisation.

Previously, I've worked across marketplaces (Daraz), digital marketing analytics, and strategy, giving me a multidisciplinary lens that helps me understand business problems from multiple angles.

I'm passionate about using data to help organisations make smarter, faster decisions, whether that's forecasting, dashboarding, deep-diving into business performance, or analysing customer behaviour. My long-term goal is to grow into an AI-enabled analyst who can work across analytics, data science, and data engineering.

If data tells a story, I want to be the one who uncovers it.
